What's the biggest obstacle in wheel sales? The question running through your customer's head: "I wonder how that would look on my car?"

The customer walks into your shop, looks at the wheels on display, likes what they see, but can't quite commit. They say "let me think about it" and leave. Return rates are low, because deciding based on imagination alone is hard — especially when thousands of dollars are on the line.

What if you could show your customer their favorite wheel mounted on their own car, from a photo on their phone, in about a minute?

## The Psychology of Sales: See It, Buy It

People trust what they see. Watching a product in use speeds up the buying decision. Furniture retailers have leaned on this for years — AR apps that show "how would this sofa look in your living room?" have driven real sales increases.

The same principle applies to wheels. Once a customer sees the wheel on their own car, saying "yes, that looks great" becomes a lot easier.

## The WheelVision Business Solution

**In-store use:** When a customer visits your shop, they snap a photo of their car with their phone, pick the wheel they like, and see the result in about a minute. Instead of "let me think about it," they say "I'll take it."
