## How to Take the Best Photo for a Wheel Try-On

For the most realistic results, keep these tips in mind when shooting your photo:

**Get close** — Shoot from a distance where the wheel, fender, and tire are all clearly visible. Instead of a wide shot of the whole car, focus on the wheel area.

**Good lighting** — Shoot outdoors in daylight, or under showroom lighting. Avoid dark or heavily shadowed settings.

**Sharp, not blurry** — A blurry photo makes the AI's job harder. Keep your phone steady.

**Straight angle** — Shoot the wheel from the side or at a slight angle. Avoid extreme high or low angles.
