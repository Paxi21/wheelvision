Buying new wheels is exciting for car owners — but also stressful. A set of wheels can run anywhere from a few hundred to a few thousand dollars. After paying that, the last thing you want is to think "another design would've looked better."

So is there a way to see how wheels will look on your car before you buy? There is — actually, there are several.

## Method 1: Ask Your Wheel Shop for a Temporary Fitting

The classic approach. You walk into a wheel shop and ask, "can you mount this wheel on my car so I can take a look?"

Pros: you see the real wheel on your real car. Cons: it takes time, every wheel needs to be swapped on and off again, you wait if the shop is busy, and you can only try wheels the shop actually has in stock.

## Method 2: 3D Wheel Configurators

There are various sites online where you pick your car's make and model and virtually try on wheels.

Pros: fast and free. Cons: limited to the vehicle models in their database. Your car's color, modifications, and surroundings aren't reflected — everyone sees the same stock image. If your car isn't listed, you can't try it. And most of these sites are no longer actively maintained.

## Method 3: Editing in Photoshop

Dropping a photo of your car into Photoshop and manually compositing the wheel. Some wheel dealers offer this as a service.

Pros: you see it on your actual photo. Cons: requires Photoshop skills or money, a fresh edit is needed for every wheel, and details like shadows and reflections are usually missing. Most importantly — it's slow. A single image can take hours to prepare.

## Method 4: AI Wheel Try-On (The Newest Method)

As of 2026, AI has automated this entire process. Platforms like WheelVision feed your car photo to an AI model, which shows your selected wheel mounted on your car within roughly a minute.

Pros: your real photo, any wheel, about 1 minute, shadows and reflections handled automatically. Cons: you need an internet connection, and AI results aren't always perfect — but in most cases they're more realistic than a Photoshop edit.

## Tips for Choosing the Right Wheels

Whichever method you use, keep these in mind when picking wheels:

**Match your car's character** — Aggressive off-road wheels on a sedan, or thin sporty wheels on an SUV, tend to look off. Choose a wheel style that fits your car's personality.

**Color harmony** — Bright or chrome wheels stand out a lot on dark-colored cars. Black wheels look sharp on white or gray cars. But don't be afraid to break the rules — gold wheels can look stunning on a black car.

**Wheel size** — Bigger isn't always better. Oversized wheels reduce ride comfort and raise tire costs. We recommend going no more than 1-2 inches above the original wheel size.

**Bolt pattern** — The wheel's bolt count and spacing must match your car. Your wheel dealer will know this, but it's worth learning your car's bolt pattern too.
